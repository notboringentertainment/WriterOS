Written by Morgan from canon·Approved Oct 13·2,140 words

Canon changed after this was approved. Two facts moved: Marta's age, and the depot closing in act three.

Dell Avery has towed cars off the same eleven miles of interstate for nineteen years. He knows every exit by its accidents. When the depot's owner dies and leaves the business to his daughter Marta, Dell expects to be let go. Instead she hands him the keys and a ledger that doesn't add up.

Act one

The night shift is Dell's whole life: the radio, the coffee, the calls that come in after two. Marta wants the depot sold by spring. Dell wants one more winter. They agree on nothing except that the numbers are wrong, and that somebody in town has been paying the depot to look away.

Act two

A wreck on the overpass leaves a car nobody will claim. Dell tows it in, and the paperwork leads back to the bank that holds Marta's loan. He starts asking questions the town has spent years not asking.
