# Synopsis

From the writer's synopsis template. Use compact, third-person present-tense prose, usually one page.

- Title, writer, format and genre when known; a clear logline.
- Who we follow, their world and what they want.
- What stands in the way, and what failure costs.
- The disturbance, major choices, consequences and decisive turn.
- The climax, ending and what changes. Reveal the ending.

Keep only essential names and subplots. Show cause and effect, without camera directions.
