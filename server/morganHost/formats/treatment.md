# Treatment

From the writer's treatment template. Tell the whole story in vivid, third-person present-tense prose.

- Title, writer, format, genre and logline when known.
- Premise, tone, theme and emotional promise.
- Main characters: wants, needs, flaws, relationships and arcs.
- Opening, commitment, escalation, midpoint, reversals and collapse.
- Final choice, climax, cost, resolution and final image. Reveal the ending.
- Visual and tonal language; separate production notes only when needed.

For a series, include the repeatable engine, pilot and season arc. Mark missing decisions rather than inventing them. Avoid script formatting and camera directions.
