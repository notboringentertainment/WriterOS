# Outline

From the writer's outline template. Make the causal structure clear before drafting scenes.

- Title, format, genre and story spine when known.
- Protagonist, goal, opposition, stakes and transformation.
- Opening, inciting incident, commitment, midpoint, lowest point, climax and ending.
- Ordered acts, sequences or beats: who is present, what happens, conflict, turn, consequence and why the next beat follows.
- For a series: repeatable engine, season arc, episode map and ending hooks.

Every beat changes the situation. Follow the existing beat sheet; do not script dialogue or step-by-step action. Keep production notes separate.
