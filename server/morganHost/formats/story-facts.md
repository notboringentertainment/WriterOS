# Story Facts

From the writer's story-bible template. Preserve the project's creative rules and continuity.

- Identity and premise: title, format, genre, logline, hook and audience promise.
- Tone and style: pacing, visual language, sound and limits.
- World: setting, rules, power, history and mythology.
- Characters: wants, needs, flaws, secrets, arcs, relationships, behavior and voice.
- Story engine and structure: feature arc or pilot, season and future arcs.
- Continuity facts, sources and open questions; production references when needed.

Keep established facts separate from unresolved decisions. This text does not change canon without the writer's explicit yes.
